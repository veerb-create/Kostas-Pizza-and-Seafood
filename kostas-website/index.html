Our Story

Three decades of feeding Needham, one pita at a time.

Kosta's opened its doors on Chestnut Street with a simple promise: real ingredients, generous portions, and the warm welcome of a family kitchen. Nearly thirty years later, that promise hasn't changed.

We bake our pita fresh every day, hand-toss our pizzas, and bring in our seafood from New England waters. No MSG, no preservatives — just good food made the way we'd cook for our own family.
